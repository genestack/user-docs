# Save studies to bookmarks

Save studies to your bookmarks so you can return to them quickly.

1. In the Study Browser, click the three-dot menu to the left of a study title, then click **Save to bookmarks**.

   ![Bookmark studies](../assets/user-guide/doc-odm-user-guide/doc-odm-user-guide/images/11Bookmarks.png)

2. To view your bookmarked studies, click the **Saved** icon. This section displays your studies and those shared within the groups you belong to.

   ![Bookmarked studies](../assets/user-guide/doc-odm-user-guide/doc-odm-user-guide/images/12Bookmarked2.png)

3. To remove a bookmark, open the **Saved** view and remove the study from there.

!!! note

    Studies are shared with groups, not with individual users. See [Sharing and ownership](../overview/access-control/sharing-and-ownership.md) for how group-based access works.
