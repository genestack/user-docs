# Platform security

Genestack operates an ISO27001:2022 certified Information Security Management System (ISMS). The documents below underpin Genestack's security management.

| Document name | Purpose | Owner | Last updated |
|---|---|---|---|
| ISO27001 Certificate | Validates Genestack's ISMS and information security compliance. | Chief Executive Officer | 1 May 2026 |
| ISO27017 & ISO27018 Certificate | Validates Genestack's cloud service provider and personal information security and privacy compliance. | Chief Executive Officer | 1 May 2026 |
| ODM High Level Design | ODM platform high level design document, covering all aspects of design including security management. | Head of Engineering | 13 October 2025 |
| ODM Security Requirements | Describes the detailed security control requirements applied to the ODM platform. | Head of Engineering | 3 July 2026 |
| ODM Security Responsibilities Matrix | Describes the responsibility for data security and privacy controls applied to ODM as an on-premise, Virtual Private Cloud (VPC), or Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) deployment. | Head of Engineering | 3 July 2026 |
| Information Security Policy | Defines Genestack's high level security objectives. | Chief Executive Officer | 12 May 2025 |
| Operational Security Policy | Defines the policy for the operational security controls that underpin the development of Genestack products, service delivery, and the protection of the Genestack production environment. | Head of Engineering | 2 March 2026 |
| Cloud Service Provider Policy | Defines cloud-specific control requirements aligned with ISO/IEC 27017:2025, supporting secure service delivery and data protection. | Head of Engineering | 14 October 2025 |
| Terms of Reference for the Security Working Group | Defines how all aspects of Genestack's ISO27001 ISMS are managed, governed, and audited. | Head of Engineering | 6 May 2025 |
| Software Development Life Cycle Policy | Establishes a policy for an SDLC framework and related software application development methodologies and tools. | Head of Engineering | 6 March 2026 |
| Access Control Policy | Addresses the security requirements for all user login accounts on Genestack information assets by Genestack staff. | Head of Engineering | 25 March 2026 |
| Acceptable Use Policy | Defines individuals' obligations to protect Genestack systems and information from internal and external threats, in order to ensure competitive advantage and business continuity. | Head of Engineering | 13 February 2026 |
| Artificial Intelligence (AI) Policy | Defines the controlled use of AI models and tools within Genestack, setting out what colleagues may and may not use and the restrictions that apply, while taking advantage of improvements to operational efficiency. | Head of Engineering | 13 August 2026 |

This page was last updated in August 2026.
